Choosing Your Class

In Classic World of Warcraft, the choice of class is one of the most important decisions a player makes. Each class offers a unique playstyle, role, and set of abilities that influence how you approach combat, group content, and exploration. Whether you prefer dealing damage, supporting allies, or tanking fearsome enemies, the class you choose shapes your entire experience in Azeroth.

Beyond class abilities, your character’s race provides additional bonuses that can enhance your strengths or complement your playstyle. From the stamina boosts of Orcs to the critical strike bonuses of Night Elves, racial abilities add another layer of strategy when selecting a character. Choosing the right combination of class and race can make a significant difference in leveling efficiency, PvP encounters, and endgame performance.

This page breaks down all available classes in Classic WoW, highlighting their strengths, weaknesses, and racial synergies. Use this guide to explore your options, compare playstyles, and find the perfect hero for your adventures in Azeroth.

Alliance Races:

  • Human
    • Diplomacy: Reputation gains increased by 10%.
    • Perception: Dramatically increases stealth detection for 20 sec.
    • The Human Spirit: Spirit increased by 5%.
    • Sword and Mace Specialization: Skill with swords and maces increased by 5.
  • Night Elf
    • Nature Resistance: Nature Resistance increased by 10.
    • Wisp Spirit: Transform into a wisp upon death, increasing movement speed by 50%.
    • Quickness: Dodge chance increased by 1%.
    • Shadowmeld: Activate to slip into the shadows, reducing the chance for enemies to detect your presence. Lasts until cancelled or upon moving. Night Elf Rogues and Druids with Shadowmeld are more difficult to detect while stealthed or prowling.
  • Dwarf
    • Find Treasure: Allows the dwarf to sense nearby treasure, making it appear on the minimap.
    • Frost Resistance: Increases Frost Resistance by 10.
    • Gun Specialization: Guns skill increased by 5.
    • Stoneform: While active, grants immunity to Bleed, Poison, and Disease effects. In addition, Armor increased by 10%. Lasts 8 sec.
  • Gnome
    • Arcane Resistance: Arcane Resistance increased.
    • Expansive Mind: Intelligence increased by 5%.
    • Engineering Specialization: Engineering skill increased by 15.
    • Escape Artist: Escape the effects of any immobilization or movement speed reduction effect.

Horde Races

  • Orc
    • Axe Specialization: Skill with Axes and Two-Handed Axes increased by 5.
    • Blood Fury: Increases base melee attack power by 25% for 15 sec and reduces healing effects on you by 50% for 25 sec.
    • Command: Damage dealt by Hunter and Warlock pets increased by 5%.
    • Hardiness: Chance to resist Stun effects increased by an additional 25%.
  • Troll
    • Beast Slaying: Damage dealt versus Beasts increased by 5%.
    • Berserking: Increases your casting and attack speed by 10% to 30%. At full health the speed increase is 10% with a greater effect up to 30% if you are badly hurt when you activate Berserking. Lasts 10 sec.
    • Bow Specialization: Skill with Bow Weapons increased by 5.
    • Regeneration: Health regeneration rate increased by 10%. 10% of total Health regeneration may continue during combat.
    • Throwing Specialization: Skill with Throwing Weapons increased by 5.
  • Tauren
    • Cultivation: Herbalism skill increased by 15.
    • Endurance: Total Health increased by 5%.
    • Nature Resistance: Nature Resistance increased by 10.
    • War Stomp: Stuns up to 5 enemies within 8 yds for 2 sec.
  • Undead
    • Cannibalize: When activated, regenerates 7% of total health every 2 sec for 10 sec. Only works on Humanoid or Undead corpses within 5 yds. Any movement, action, or damage taken while Cannibalizing will cancel the effect.
    • Shadow Resistance: Shadow Resistance increased by 10.
    • Underwater Breathing: Underwater breath lasts 300% longer than normal.
    • Will of the Forsaken: Provides immunity to Charm, Fear and Sleep while active. May also be used while already afflicted by Charm, Fear or Sleep. Lasts 5 sec.

Warrior

Pros:

  • Strong tanking and melee damage potential
  • High survivability with proper gear
  • Essential for raids and PvP frontlines

Cons:

  • Dependent on gear for maximum effectiveness
  • Limited ranged options
  • Resource management (rage) can be tricky for beginners

Paladin

Pros:

  • Excellent support class (healing, buffs)
  • Durable in solo and group play
  • Strong against undead and demons

Cons:

  • Slower leveling compared to DPS classes
  • Limited mobility and ranged abilities
  • Complex rotation to optimize effectiveness

Shaman

Pros:

  • Versatile hybrid: can heal, DPS (melee or ranged), and provide utility
  • Totems provide buffs, crowd control, and strategic advantages
  • Strong in groups and PvP due to supportive abilities

Cons:

  • Totem management can be complex for new players
  • Limited gear choices can reduce effectiveness in soloing
  • Damage output is lower than pure DPS classes without careful planning

Hunter

Pros:

  • Strong ranged DPS and solo capabilities
  • Pet provides additional tanking and utility
  • Good mobility and kiting abilities

Cons:

  • Pet management can be challenging for new players
  • Lower effectiveness in melee situations
  • Ammo and gear dependent

Rogue

Pros:

  • High burst melee damage and stealth utility
  • Excellent for PvP and crowd control
  • Can lockpick and disarm traps

Cons:

  • Fragile when caught out of stealth
  • Requires energy management and timing for full effectiveness
  • Dependent on positioning and tactics

Priest

Pros:

  • Powerful healing and support capabilities
  • Strong PvP utility with crowd control and buffs
  • Decent ranged DPS (Shadow spec)

Cons:

  • Low survivability when soloing
  • Mana management is critical
  • Shadow DPS spec requires careful gear selection

Mage

Pros:

  • High ranged DPS and crowd control
  • Strong utility with portals and food/water creation
  • Good for farming and leveling in groups

Cons:

  • Very fragile; low health and armor
  • Dependent on mana management
  • Requires careful positioning in combat

Warlock

Pros:

  • Powerful damage over time (DoT) spells and curses
  • Summons pets to assist in combat
  • Utility with healthstones, soulstones, and crowd control

Cons:

  • Dependent on pet management and positioning
  • Slower cast times; fragile without pet support
  • Complex rotation to optimize DoTs

Druid

Pros:

  • Versatile hybrid: can tank, heal, or DPS
  • Shapeshifting offers mobility and utility
  • Strong soloing capabilities

Cons:

  • Jack-of-all-trades but master-of-none in early game
  • Requires knowledge of multiple roles and forms
  • Gear choices can be complex due to hybrid nature
